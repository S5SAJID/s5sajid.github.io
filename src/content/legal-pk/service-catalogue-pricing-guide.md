## PART G: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I start with a Landing Page and upgrade to a SaaS later?**
A: Yes, but understand that upgrading is not the same as adding to. Moving from a static site to a full-stack application often requires rebuilding from scratch, not just adding features. Starting at the right tier saves money long-term.

**Q: Why is the price a range and not a fixed number?**
A: Because every project is unique. A 5-page website with complex animations costs more than a 5-page website with standard design. I give you an exact fixed quote after our discovery session. The ranges tell you what budget tier to plan for.

**Q: Do you offer payment plans?**
A: Yes. Payments are split across project milestones — deposit upfront, then at 50% completion, then at final delivery. This ensures you never pay for work that hasn't been done.

**Q: What if I want something that's between two tiers?**
A: That's the most common situation. After our discovery call, I'll give you an exact quote that reflects exactly what you need — it may fall anywhere within or between the stated ranges.

**Q: What if my project grows beyond what we agreed?**
A: Any additions to the original scope are quoted separately and approved by you in writing before work begins. You control what you spend.

**Q: Can I change my mind after we start?**
A: You can always request changes. However, depending on what has already been built, changes may require additional time and cost. Early-stage changes are cheaper than late-stage ones. Tell me everything upfront.

**Q: What if I'm not happy with the final product?**
A: If the product does not match the agreed scope, I will fix it within the included revision hours at no charge. If your dissatisfaction is due to a change in personal preference (you want something different than what you agreed to), that is a new request billed at post-MVP rates.
