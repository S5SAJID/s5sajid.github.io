## PART I — DEFINITIONS

### Article 1: Definitions

The following terms, when used in this Agreement, shall carry the meanings set forth below. These definitions apply throughout this Agreement and all associated documents.

**1.1 "Agreement"** means this Master Services Agreement, together with any Project Scope Document, invoice, proposal, quotation, or written communication that explicitly references and incorporates this Agreement.

**1.2 "Services"** means all web development, software engineering, technical consulting, deployment assistance, integration services, and any other professional services provided by the Provider to the Client as described in Part II of this Agreement.

**1.3 "Project"** means a discrete, defined body of work agreed upon by both parties, the scope of which is established through a Project Scope Document, proposal, or written communication.

**1.4 "MVP" (Minimum Viable Product)** means the initial, functional version of the Project comprising only those features explicitly agreed upon in the Project Scope Document. The MVP does not include any features, pages, workflows, or integrations not expressly listed in the agreed scope, regardless of how similar they are to included features.

**1.5 "Project Scope Document"** means the written document, proposal, or communication — which may take the form of a proposal document, email chain, prototype video, wireframe, or feature list — that defines the exact boundaries of the Project, including all features, technology stack, and deliverables.

**1.6 "Deliverable"** means any tangible output produced by the Provider in connection with the Services, including but not limited to source code, design files, documentation, configuration files, and deployed applications.

**1.7 "Intellectual Property" or "IP"** means all inventions, designs, code, databases, documentation, trade secrets, methods, and any other proprietary works created in connection with the Services.

**1.8 "Scope Creep"** means any request by the Client for features, functionality, pages, integrations, design changes, or any other work that was not explicitly included in the agreed Project Scope Document, regardless of how minor such a request may appear.

**1.9 "Revision"** means a modification or correction to a Deliverable that falls strictly within the boundaries of the agreed Project Scope Document. A Revision is not the addition of new features, redesigns, or preference-based aesthetic changes.

**1.10 "Serious Bug"** means a defect in the Provider's Deliverable that renders a core, explicitly agreed-upon feature completely non-functional for all or substantially all users, including but not limited to: (a) complete failure of the authentication/login system; (b) complete failure of the payment/checkout system; (c) critical data loss or data not persisting to the database; (d) confirmed security vulnerability exposing user data; (e) complete failure of an entire agreed feature module; (f) database connection failure causing site-wide 500 errors; or (g) rendering the application entirely inaccessible. A Serious Bug does not include: minor visual discrepancies, performance preferences, single-user issues, issues caused by the Client's hosting environment, issues caused by third-party service providers, or any issue where a reasonable workaround exists.

**1.11 "Working Day"** means Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time (PST/PKT), excluding public holidays observed in Pakistan and any holidays declared by the Provider with reasonable notice.

**1.12 "Deposit"** means the non-refundable upfront payment required before the Provider commences any work on a Project, the amount of which is determined by the Client relationship tier as defined in Article 7.

**1.13 "Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by either party to the other, whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

**1.14 "Force Majeure Event"** means any event beyond a party's reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, acts of war, civil unrest, government action, pandemics, prolonged internet or power outages, or failure of third-party infrastructure providers.

**1.15 "Pakistani Rupee" or "PKR"** means the lawful currency of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, the exclusive currency in which all monetary obligations under this Agreement are denominated.

## PART IV — REVISIONS AND POST-LAUNCH SUPPORT

### Article 7: Revision Policy

**7.1 Included Revisions.** The fixed project price includes one (1) round of revisions, comprising a maximum of four (4) to five (5) cumulative hours of revision work. These revisions must:
- (a) Be requested within seven (7) calendar days of MVP delivery;
- (b) Pertain exclusively to defects, errors, or deviations from the agreed Project Scope Document;
- (c) Not constitute preference-based aesthetic changes, new features, or redesigns.

**7.2 What Constitutes a Valid Revision.** The following are valid revision requests: factual errors in implemented logic, broken links or navigation errors, features that do not behave as shown in the approved prototype, mobile responsiveness issues present across all major devices, and browser compatibility issues on current versions of Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.

**7.3 What Does Not Constitute a Revision.** The following do not constitute revisions and are billable under Article 3.3: color or font preference changes, layout preference changes, changes to agreed copy or content, requests to add or remove features, changing the agreed technology or architecture, and any request prefaced with "Can we also…" or "While you're at it…"

**7.4 Revision Hour Tracking.** The Provider shall track all revision time and notify the Client when three (3) hours of the included revision budget have been consumed. Upon exhausting the included revision hours, the Provider shall cease revision work and notify the Client. Continuation of revision work beyond the included hours requires the Client's written approval and is billed at the applicable per-request rate under Article 3.3.

**7.5 Revision Window.** Revision requests submitted after seven (7) calendar days of MVP delivery are not covered by the included revision allocation and shall be billed as post-MVP requests under Article 3.3 regardless of their nature.



### Article 8: Post-Launch Support

**8.1 Serious Bug Support Window.** For a period of thirty (30) calendar days following the delivery of the MVP ("Support Window"), the Provider shall remedy Serious Bugs as defined in Article 1.10 at no additional charge, subject to the limitations set forth in this Article.

**8.2 Free Support Hour Limit.** The Provider's obligation to provide free Serious Bug remediation is limited to a cumulative maximum of two (2) to three (3) hours of support time during the Support Window. Once this limit is reached, all further support — including Serious Bug remediation — is subject to per-request billing under Article 3.3.

**8.3 Hour Tracking and Notification.** The Provider shall track all support time and notify the Client in writing when two (2) hours of the free support allocation have been consumed. Upon reaching the limit, the Provider shall provide a written estimate before performing any additional work.

**8.4 Excluded from Free Support.** The following are expressly excluded from free support, regardless of how they are characterized by the Client:
- (a) Issues caused by the Client's modifications to the code after delivery
- (b) Issues caused by third-party service providers (hosting, email, payment processors)
- (c) Issues caused by the Client's failure to maintain agreed hosting or infrastructure
- (d) Issues affecting only a single user that cannot be reproduced by the Provider
- (e) Performance-related complaints where the application remains functional
- (f) Any feature request or design change, however minor

**8.5 Post-Support-Window Billing.** After the thirty (30) day Support Window expires, all support — including Serious Bug remediation — is billed at the applicable per-request rate under Article 3.3 without exception.

**8.6 No Ongoing Maintenance Obligation.** The Provider is under no obligation to provide any ongoing maintenance, updates, library upgrades, security patches, or monitoring services beyond what is expressly set forth in this Article. The Client assumes full responsibility for the long-term maintenance of their product upon expiry of the Support Window.
